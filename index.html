1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Smart Expense Manager mobile application (the "App"), package identifier com.aistudio.smartexpense.kpztqx, developed and operated by the developer ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning data handling, storage, permissions, and advertising to comply with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy legislation.

2. Zero Banking Credentials Policy

Strict Financial Protection Guarantee: Smart Expense Manager is a personal offline ledger. We NEVER request, access, collect, transmit, or store sensitive banking passwords, Internet banking credentials, UPI PINs, ATM debit/credit card PINs, CVV codes, or One-Time Passwords (OTPs).

Payment method tags (such as UPI, Cash, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer) chosen within the application are strictly descriptive labels for your own personal accounting.

3. Information Collected & Managed

All data managed inside the application is provided voluntarily by you for budgeting and personal bookkeeping purposes:

  • Expense & Income Records: Amounts, transaction dates, titles, custom categories, payment method labels, and optional user notes.
  • Loan & EMI Details: Loan names, financial institution names, total installments, paid installments, monthly EMI amount, and due dates.
  • Personal Lending & Borrowing: Contact name, optional phone number, amount lent or borrowed, expected repayment dates, and notes.
  • Staff & Worker Ledgers: Worker name, job title, daily/monthly wage amounts, advance payments, and payment history.
  • Travel Expenses: Trip titles, destinations, allocated budget, and categorized travel expenses.

4. Local Storage & Zero Server Sync

100% On-Device Storage: All your financial calculations and ledger records are stored entirely on your local device using a private, sandboxed SQLite Room database.

We do not operate any proprietary cloud servers, remote databases, or sync APIs. Your financial ledger data is never transmitted to our developers or any third-party data broker.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob SDK)

To keep the application free for all users, Smart Expense Manager integrates with Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements (Banner and Interstitial ads).

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain device and diagnostic data in accordance with Google's policies, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address, and general device specifications (device model, operating system version).
  • Performance & Analytics: Ad impression data, click metrics, crash logs, and diagnostic telemetry for ad fraud prevention and delivery optimization.

For further details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies & Policy

You can manage personalized advertising preferences on your Android device anytime by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete advertising ID.

6. Device Permissions

The application requests only minimal, necessary Android system permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional on Android 13+ (API 33+). Used exclusively to deliver local reminders for upcoming EMI due dates, bill due dates, and budget threshold alerts. You can toggle reminders on or off at any time in the in-app Settings menu.

7. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights

Because all records reside locally on your device, you retain total custody and control over your data:

  • Data Export: You can generate and export complete consolidated financial reports in CSV format at any time directly from the Reports tab.
  • Record Deletion: You can delete any individual transaction, loan, worker, or trip at any time.
  • Complete Eradication: You can wipe all app data immediately by clearing the app storage in your Android system settings (Settings → Apps → Smart Expense Manager → Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the application.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Smart Expense Manager is designed for general audiences and individuals managing personal finances and household budgets. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

9. Security Measures

We prioritize the security of your financial data:

  • Local data is stored in the private application sandbox provided by the Android OS, preventing other unauthorized apps from accessing your database.
  • No sensitive financial access credentials or bank passwords are ever handled.
